BEYOND FORM
ما وراء الشكل

Thirty-one wood, rope and mirror works, each carrying a single idea discovered in its own form — not decoration, but a position held quietly, one piece at a time.

Pieces31
MediumWood · Rope · Mirror
LanguageEN / AR
On the Collection

Every piece here stages a moment of crossing — from raw material to carved form, from wholeness to fracture, from a surface to what waits behind it. BEYOND FORM is not a look. It is the condition all thirty-one works share.
